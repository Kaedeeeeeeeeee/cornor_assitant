```text
Corner Peek 是一款 macOS 菜单栏工具，把搜索、网页和常用网站收进屏幕边缘。需要时轻轻唤出，用完后自动收起，让桌面保持清爽。
```

## Privacy Policy 摘要

- Corner Peek 不要求账号登录。

### Content Rights / Third-Party Content

Apple 对“包含、显示或访问第三方内容”的 App 要求开发者拥有必要权利，或在各销售地区法律下有其他允许依据。Corner Peek 的建议口径：

- Bundled app assets, icon, landing copy, screenshots: owned by developer or created for Corner Peek.
- App does not bundle, mirror, cache, or redistribute third-party articles, videos, images, or databases.
- App can access third-party websites only when users type a URL, search, click a result, or open a pinned site.
- If asked whether the app contains, shows, or accesses third-party content: answer Yes, because Corner Peek is a browser-like WebKit app.
- If asked whether you have rights or are otherwise permitted: answer Yes only on the basis that content is user-directed web access and not bundled/rehosted by Corner Peek; account holder should make the final legal confirmation.

Suggested explanation:

```text
Corner Peek is a browser-like macOS utility. It does not bundle, mirror, cache, or redistribute third-party content. Users choose the websites they open by entering URLs, searching, or using pinned sites. Bundled app assets and product metadata are owned by the developer or created for Corner Peek.
```
